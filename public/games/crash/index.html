Rocket Crash
  1. Place one bet in each panel, or pre-bet during the previous flight.
  2. The multiplier climbs from 1.00× while the rocket is in the air.
  3. Cash out before the rocket flies away. Your payout is stake × cash-out multiplier.
  4. Set an auto cash-out and the server settles it at exactly your target if the rocket reaches it.
  5. Crash shows nothing about the next round. Every result is committed before betting opens.

Each round publishes a SHA-256 commitment before betting opens. The server seed, client seed and round nonce produce the crash point with HMAC-SHA-256.

After the crash the full server seed is revealed, so you can recompute the result in your browser.

The commitment proves the outcome was fixed before bets were accepted. It does not prove how the seed was chosen. A hash chain or external beacon is a future owner decision.
