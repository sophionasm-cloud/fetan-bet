How to play

Take penalties against the keeper and climb the multiplier ladder. Bank your winnings whenever you like — one save ends the run.

  • Aim with the arrow keys, or tap a third of the goal. The green arrows around the ball also work.
  • Shoot with Space, the SHOOT button, or by tapping the ball.
  • Watch the keeper's lean and glove position before you shoot — that is his tell.
  • Every goal moves you up the ladder. CLAIM after any goal to bank bet × multiplier.
  • A save ends the run and loses the stake. Difficulty changes how reliable the keeper's tell is.
