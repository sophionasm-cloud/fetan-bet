How to play

Place a bet and guide the chicken across the road. Every successful hop raises your multiplier through the approved Easy, Medium or Hard levels. The server commits each level result before the round; traffic animation is visual presentation and does not reveal the result.

  • Play starts the round and deducts your bet from your Fetan Bet wallet.
  • GO (or Space) advances one level.
  • Cash out (or C) banks bet × current multiplier at any time after the first level.
  • Finishing the road pays bet × the final multiplier. There is no extra completion bonus.
  • Harder difficulty has fewer, riskier levels and larger approved multipliers.
